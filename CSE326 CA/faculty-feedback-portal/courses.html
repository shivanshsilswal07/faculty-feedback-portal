Criteria

What students are asked to rate

  • Teaching effectiveness — did the sessions help you understand the subject?
  • Explanation clarity — were concepts explained in an understandable way?
  • Subject knowledge — depth and accuracy of the faculty's command of the topic.
  • Interaction — approachability and willingness to engage with students.
  • Use of examples — practical illustrations that connect theory to application.
  • Course material — quality of slides, notes and practice resources.
  • Punctuality — classes starting and ending as scheduled.
  • Doubt clarification — responsiveness to questions inside and outside class.
