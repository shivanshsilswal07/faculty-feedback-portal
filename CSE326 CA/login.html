Why login?

Logging in lets the portal:

  • Show your dashboard status for each course
  • Pre-fill your Student ID for identified feedback
  • Apply the duplicate-submission check correctly

Choosing Anonymous mode later on the feedback form still hides your identity from displayed results, even if you're logged in.
